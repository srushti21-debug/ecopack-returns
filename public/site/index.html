Return. Reuse. Recycle.

EcoPack makes it effortless for customers to send back the boxes, mailers and fillers from their online orders. Every returned package is inspected, reused where possible, and recycled when it isn't — keeping packaging waste out of landfills.

How It Works

Four simple steps to a returned package

No printing, no complicated forms — just save the packaging and let EcoPack handle the rest.

01
📦

Order Delivered

Your online order arrives in EcoPack-tagged packaging with a unique return ID.

02
🧺

Save the Packaging

Flatten the box and keep the fillers instead of throwing them into the bin.

03
🗓️

Schedule a Return

Pick a pickup slot or drop it at the nearest EcoPack collection point.

04
♻️

Reuse or Recycle

We inspect, sanitise and re-circulate it — or recycle the material responsibly.

Benefits

Why brands and customers choose EcoPack

A return programme that is good for the planet and simple for everyone involved.

🗑️

Reduce Packaging Waste

Divert cardboard, mailers and fillers away from landfill after a single use.

🔁

Encourage Reuse

⚡

Easy Returns

Track Your Impact
